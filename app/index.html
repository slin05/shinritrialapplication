Detective cheat sheet from the Shinri Trial guidebook

    

      

        
Investigation timer

        
7 min at the start, +2 min per opened floor, capped at 15.

        

          
          
          7 min
        

      

      

        
Body discovery

        
    
          
  • Counts once 3 different characters look at it. The killer can be one of them, but never first, and only after 20 real seconds.
    • 
          
  • Auto-alert if nobody finds it within 5 real minutes.
    • 
          
  • Monokuma File time of death is accurate to ±1 in-game minute.
    • 
        

      

      

        
Fingerprints

        
    
          
  • Six kinds: ordinary, smeared, bloody, bloody-smeared, tar, tar-smeared. Only ordinary and bloody point to a person.
    • 
          
  • Ordinary and smeared need a UV flashlight to see. They last 18 in-game hours; bloody and tar ones last 36.
    • 
          
  • Crowbar break-ins leave no door prints but make a loud noise.
    • 
          
  • Trap prints belong to whoever set it, or whoever last picked it up.
    • 
        

      

      

        
Gloves & bloody hands

        
    
          
  • Reusable gloves smear every print. Disposable ones smear for 10 uses, then tear and leave normal prints.
    • 
          
  • "Bloody hands" only appears from a melee hit before the investigation, never from dragging, punching or picking up a weapon.
    • 
          
  • Hitting in gloves bloodies the gloves, not the hands. Bloody gloves can't be washed in a sink, only wiped with a rag.
    • 
        

      

      

        
Shoeprints

        
    
          
  • Bloody (blood puddle), wet (bathhouse or pool), dirty (either garden, or the dirty oil at the workbench next to the gym).
    • 
          
  • They last 10 steps, and those steps fade after 12 in-game hours or 10 real minutes. UV shows them best.
    • 
        

      

      

        
Sinks (where they wash up)

        
Restrooms by the gym and dormitory, abandoned floor, 2F, 4F, medical office, laundry, kitchen, dorm rooms, art room back room, chemistry lab.

      

      

        
Environmental deaths

        
    
          
  • Freezer: about 3:31 real time. Sauna: about 2:28. The killer is whoever shut the door.
    • 
          
  • Starved or sleepless while restrained: whoever applied the restraints.
    • 
          
  • Pushed from a height: whoever released the victim, not whoever tied them up.
    • 
        

      

      

        
Rules that narrow suspects

        
    
          
  • Max 2 kills per killer (or killer + accomplice) per chapter. 3+ bodies means at least two separate killers.
    • 
          
  • Killing with more than one witness is forbidden, so a group of 3+ is safe.
    • 
          
  • A tied-up "alibi" is weak: killers can bind themselves, but can only remove a bag on their own.
    • 
          
  • Hits after death leave no wounds. Sleep effects are always listed after injuries.
    • 
        

      

      

        
Repair stages (log their times)

        
    
          
  • 1F electrical panel → 2F supercomputer → 3F air purifier → 4F escape capsules.
    • 
          
  • Opening times catch fake alibis: nobody was "by the boiler room" before it opened.
    • 
          
  • The 3F utility room links the gym bridge, 3F, the library archive vent, the 2F men's restroom, the pool and the boiler room.
    • 
        

      

      

        
Monokuma events

        
    
          
  • Masquerade: everyone gets a disguise. A disguise without gloves leaves smeared bloody prints.
    • 
          
  • Blackout: lights out across the whole academy.
    • 
          
  • Teleport: everyone sent to random spots.
    • 
        

      

    

  


  

    
Quick Match rules summarized from blog.shinri-trial.com

    

      

        
Kill limit & accomplices

        
    
          
  • Max 2 kills per chapter, shared between you and your accomplice. Indirect help (handing over an explosive, etc.) counts.
    • 
          
  • Only one accomplice allowed.
    • 
          
  • The limit is lifted during the Escape final quest.
    • 
        

      

      

        
Public killing

        
    
          
  • Solo: no killing with 2+ witnesses who aren't accomplices in the room.
    • 
          
  • With an accomplice: 3 people present is OK if one is your accomplice. Any extra accomplice counts as a witness.
    • 
          
  • Accidental witnesses from a botched kill don't count.
    • 
        

      

      

        
Damage needs a reason (FD)

        
    
          
  • Valid: serious insults to you or someone close, direct threats, or self-defense / protecting someone.
    • 
          
  • Repeatedly hitting people during an investigation, or hitting a dead body, is NonRP.
    • 
        

      

      

        
Restraints & stuns

        
    
          
  • Need a real reason: they hurt you, seriously insulted you or a friend, threaten others, are resisting, or it's setup for a murder.
    • 
          
  • Max 5 minutes held per RP situation if it isn't progressing. Re-tying to reset the clock is a violation.
    • 
          
  • Holding someone in the bathhouse or freezer before a murder is allowed.
    • 
        

      

      

        
Powergaming (PG)

        
    
          
  • No impossible feats or ignoring mechanics.
    • 
          
  • Two armed players demanding a search: comply. Resisting a clear 2-to-1 is PG, unless the group has no RP reason and is just ganging up.
    • 
        

      

      

        
/try and /roll

        
    
          
  • Required whenever failure is possible, another player is involved, or the environment is affected.
    • 
          
  • After a fail, don't repeat the same action on the same target unless things have really changed.
    • 
          
  • Game mechanics beat /me actions (no "/me picked the lock").
    • 
        

      

      

        
Metagaming & meta-co-op

        
    
          
  • No using info from PM, SMS, LOOC, Discord or anywhere outside the match.
    • 
          
  • No teaming up based on real-life friendships or outside deals. Co-op needs an in-RP reason.
    • 
          
  • Received outside info? Don't use it and report it, and you're fine.
    • 
        

      

      

        
NonRP & leaving

        
    
          
  • Stay in character. No talking about lag, rules or real life in chat during an active scene.
    • 
          
  • Don't disconnect or go AFK mid-scene unless everyone involved agrees.
    • 
        

      

      

        
Chat & voice

        
    
          
  • English only (single phrases in other languages are fine as RP).
    • 
          
  • No voice changers, no Soundpad or similar soundboards.
    • 
        

      

      

        
Leaving & declining matches

        
    
          
  • Penalties escalate each time and reset 24h after your first one.
    • 
          
  • Leaving mid-match: warning → 30 min → 2 h → 12 h → 24 h.
    • 
          
  • Declining a found match: 2 warnings, then 3 min → 10 min → 30 min → 3 h → 24 h.
    • 
        

      

      

        
Reports & appeals

        
    
          
  • Appeals and complaints must be sent within 48 hours, or they're rejected.
    • 
          
  • Minor, harmless violations can be dismissed. Spectator reports only count for serious issues.
    • 
        

      

      

        
Third-party software

        
    
          
  • Only programs the administration has explicitly approved are allowed. Input-emulating tools, macros and client mods are a permanent ban.
    •
